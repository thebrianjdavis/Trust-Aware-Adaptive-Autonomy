# Research Questions

The framework is organized around five questions.

## 1. Reliance regulation

Can behavior-aware adaptive autonomy shift supervisor reliance in the intended
direction under simulated undertrust and overtrust?

## 2. Performance impact

Does correcting reliance behavior improve executed utility, and under which
scenario conditions?

## 3. Ablation

Is supervisor behavior feedback necessary, or can AI confidence and
machine-side trust alone produce comparable adaptation?

## 4. Sensitivity

How sensitive are results to workload, resource scarcity, task-success
uncertainty, and AI reliability?

## 5. Generalization

Which scenario regimes expose the strengths and limitations of trust-aware
adaptive autonomy?

## Working hypothesis

The current mechanism is expected to regulate reliance more consistently than
it improves utility. Behavior can move in the intended direction even when the
AI is not reliable enough for greater reliance to improve outcomes. The
[results guide](../results/index.md) explains this distinction and the limits
on its interpretation.
